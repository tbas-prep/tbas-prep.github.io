Dual Tracking with Aural Commands

Keep the rudder cursor over the lower target, keep the crosshair on the airplane, and pull the trigger whenever you hear one of your assigned letters or numbers. All three run at once. Both targets speed up as the clock runs.

◀ ▶ / pedalsRudder cursor (lower band)
W A S D / stickCrosshair (main area)
SPACE / triggerRespond to a target value

A connected joystick or gamepad is used automatically. Stick = crosshair, twist or second-device axis = rudder, button 1 = trigger. Sound on — the letter stream is spoken aloud.

Input setup

Assign your controls

Connected sticks, pedals, and gamepads appear here after you press a button or move an axis on them. The keyboard always works regardless: W A S D crosshair, ◀ ▶ rudder, Space trigger.

Briefing

Memorize your target values

Pull the trigger only when you hear one of these. Everything else in the stream is a distractor — ignore it.

They stay visible in small print at the top of the display, but reading them mid-run costs tracking time. Learn them now.
